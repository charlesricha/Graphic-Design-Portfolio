# Graphic Design Portfolio Assets

Drop your graphic design files and templates here.

### Suggested Folder Structure:
- `public/projects/`: For your portfolio design templates and project showcases (logos, posters, 3D renders, UI mockups, branding, etc.)
- `public/profile/`: For your profile portrait / avatar photo
- `public/resume.pdf`: For your downloadable CV / resume

Once you've dropped your files in either `public/` or `.public/`, let me know and I will:
1. Rename them with clean, web-friendly filenames (e.g., `brand-identity-01.png`, `poster-design.webp`).
2. Update `src/components/portfolio.tsx` and other components with your actual designs, titles, categories, and descriptions!
